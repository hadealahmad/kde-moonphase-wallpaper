Projection / registration
-------------------------
Simple cylindrical 2:1. Longitude increases eastward to the right; 0 deg
longitude (near-side sub-Earth point) is at u=0.5; north pole at v=0 and
south pole at v=1. NASA's products are already registered this way; no
column rotation is applied. Mare Imbrium (16 W) lands at u=0.456 and Mare
Crisium (59 E) at u=0.664, as expected. The mean albedo of the central
near-side band (u 0.35-0.65) is 0.88x the mean of the far-side bands
(u<0.15, u>0.85), because the near-side maria are darker; a 180-degree
rotation would invert that ratio.

Seam
----
A 6-pixel symmetric cross-fade at the 180 deg dateline (u=0/1) is applied
to the albedo and elevation before the normal is derived. Measured seam MAD
versus the typical adjacent-column MAD:

   albedo B : 0.58 / 255  vs 4.71  (ratio 0.12)
   normal G : 0.54 / 255  vs 5.12  (ratio 0.11)
   normal R : 10.75 / 255 vs 9.19  (ratio 1.17)
   packed   : 3.96 / 255  vs 6.34  (ratio 0.62)

The normal R channel is high-frequency by nature; the ratio near 1 shows
the join is not sharper than a normal column transition.
